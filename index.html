01 SYSTEM ANALYST · HEALTHCARE · AGILE DELIVERY

Benefits 24/7

Owned requirements and stakeholder management for a provider-facing benefits verification capability, enabling healthcare providers to query patient benefit coverage via natural-language search within the provider portal.

Conducted independent analysis of the underlying COBOL codebase, including associated IMS DB and DB2 table structures, to validate the business logic governing benefit eligibility — ensuring requirements reflected actual system behavior, not just stakeholder assumptions. The solution resolved provider queries against JSON-based configuration, cross-referenced with contract and benefit data maintained in Mainframe databases, delivering accurate coverage details in real time.

Managed the full Agile delivery lifecycle from design through deployment, actively participating in PI Planning, sprint ceremonies, and release activities, and coordinating development and testing teams to ensure requirements translated cleanly into production-ready functionality.

KEY ACHIEVEMENTS
  • Identified and resolved logic gaps through direct Mainframe code analysis, correcting 50+ instances of incorrect benefit lookup results for providers
  • Delivered the capability on schedule, meeting committed release timelines across Agile sprint cycles
